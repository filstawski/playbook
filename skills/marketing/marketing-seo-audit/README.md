# marketing-seo-audit — sources

## Based on

- **`seo-audit`** from
  [coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills)
  (MIT), installed in the company website and the website template; its
  audit of the company site was accepted. Restated here in our own words:
  - the audit order;
  - the title, description and heading rules;
  - the canonical and hreflang checks;
  - the structured-data caveats;
  - the local-SEO workflow (business model, profile, citations, reviews,
    location pages);
  - the rankings-push method;
  - the new-page checklist and the report format.
- **The SEO baseline the user's sites implement in code**, Sep–Nov 2026:
  prerendering, a `PAGES`-driven sitemap, self-canonicals, true JSON-LD,
  preview `noindex` by header, one-hop redirects, no analytics, and
  bilingual routing with the default language at the root.

## Dropped from upstream

- the shared marketing context file;
- "every locale under its own prefix";
- engagement metrics and analytics-based measures;
- crawl-budget and e-commerce material;
- SaaS-specific page types;
- paid-tool workflows.
