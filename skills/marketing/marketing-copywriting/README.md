# marketing-copywriting — sources

## Based on

- **`copywriting`** from
  [coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills)
  (MIT), installed in the company website and the website template and
  invoked for a full copy audit of the company site. Restated here in our own
  words:
  - the A/B/C diagnosis;
  - the leave-it-alone test;
  - the three-question line test;
  - the AI-tell categories and the self-check;
  - the honesty rules about claims;
  - the delivery format.
- **The user's own sites and concepts, Sep–Nov 2026:** German voice (*Sie*,
  identifiable *wir*), the German and English cliché lists from the company
  website's concept, fixed terms, typography, "nothing invented", and the
  structure that worked on client sites (short hero, one argument per
  section, contact-first CTAs).

## Dropped from upstream

- the shared marketing context file (the concept doc is the brief);
- proof bars, logo walls, testimonial templates, urgency, SaaS CTA
  formulas;
- page templates for pricing, enterprise and launch pages;
- the English transition phrase lists.
